    About Pathway

    Pathway helps people with different access needs compare walking routes in Singapore, and is honest about what is known and what still needs verifying.

    What the data can and can't tell you

    • Wheelchair-profile route: computed by the free Valhalla router to avoid steps recorded in OpenStreetMap. It's a computed route, not a verified accessible one: map data can be missing or out of date.
    • Not yet available: live lift status, kerb heights, slope gradients, ramps, shelter, tactile paving, rest seating and crossing safety. Pathway shows these as unknown, never as safe.
    • Barrier reports are a local demonstration. They stay in your browser and aren't shared.
    • Privacy: there's no Pathway server. Searches and route requests go directly from your browser to Photon and Valhalla. Your GPS position is used only on your device.
