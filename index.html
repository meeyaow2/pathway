About Pathway

Pathway is a prototype for accessibility-first planning. It aims to make mobility requirements visible and let people understand what is known — and what still needs verifying — about possible journeys.

Personal preferences

Step-free, shelter, lifts, rest opportunities.

Evidence-led navigation

Community input

Clearly separate observations from verified facts.

Current limitations

The basemap comes from OneMap when map tiles are reachable. Search and standard walking routes are wired to the official OneMap API, requiring user-provided credentials on the backend. The official SLA Barrier-Free Access API is subject to approval and is not connected here. Live lift status, crowdsourced moderation, offline mode, and trustworthy door-to-door accessibility scores are not implemented.

This app must not be used as a substitute for verified accessible navigation or real-time safety information.
